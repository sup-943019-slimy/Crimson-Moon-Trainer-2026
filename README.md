## 🎮 About the Game

Crimson Moon is a gothic action RPG developed and published by ProbablyMonsters. It was released on September 1, 2026, for PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. Players control a Nephilim, a human-angel hybrid, and battle demons, undead, vampires, and Hellgrowth abominations across the fallen city of Gildenarch.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Crimson Moon**. This trainer enhances the PC single-player experience by improving survivability, reducing ability downtime, and making missions and build experimentation more accessible. It is designed around Crimson Moon's melee combat, angelic powers, transformations, equipment, boons, weapon arts, replayable missions, and boss encounters.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed single-player campaign, faster build testing, easier boss encounters, or additional freedom while exploring Gildenarch and replaying missions.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move through Gildenarch normally and use the trainer after entering a mission. Movement and exploration remain controlled by the game, while defensive options can reduce the consequences of mistakes during traversal and encounters.

### Tools & Inventory

Manage weapons, armor, trinkets, boons, angels, and other build elements at the Church hub and before each hunt. Use the trainer to test different loadouts more safely, then disable combat options before changing equipment or leaving the mission.

### World & Environment

Runs begin at Sanctus Clypeus, where players prepare equipment, upgrade gear, interact with the hub, and plan missions at the War Table. Missions take place across the wards of Gildenarch and include enemies, secrets, elite encounters, mini-bosses, loot, modifiers, and final bosses.

### Combat & Weapons

Crimson Moon focuses on fast melee combat with attacks, parries, finishers, supernatural abilities, weapon arts, angelic powers, and transformations. Activate defensive or damage options before difficult encounters, and disable them when you want to preserve the intended challenge.
